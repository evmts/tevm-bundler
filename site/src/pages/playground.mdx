# Solidity import playground

This is what the Tevm bundler does to every `.sol` import, on every build and in
your editor. Step through the three panes:

<SolImportTransform />

The same pipeline runs in three places — your bundler plugin at build time, the
TypeScript plugin in your editor, and the LSP for editors without TypeScript —
so the types you hover over are exactly the types your build emits.
